# DeutschX — User Guide

A friendly walkthrough of everything DeutschX can do. No technical knowledge needed.

## Getting started

1. **Install** DeutschX (see the [README](../README.md) for the download for your computer).
2. **Open it.** On the welcome screen, paste your **Anthropic API key**
   (get one at [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys)), choose your
   **native language** and your **German level**, and click **Get started**.
3. You're in! Everything you do stays on your computer.

> **Tip:** Your API key and all your lessons live only on your machine. Nothing is uploaded.

## The layout

- A **bar down the left** switches between sections: Lessons, Vocabulary, Review, Speak, Listen,
  Words, Settings.
- A **Topic box along the top** lets you focus everything on one topic, or pick **All topics**.
  When you choose a topic, your Vocabulary, Review, Speak and Listen all show just that topic.

## 💬 Lessons

This is your tutor. Click **＋ New topic** and type what you want to learn — a short title like
*“Konjunktiv II”*, or paste a whole exercise. The tutor teaches step by step and asks you
questions.

- Type your answer and press **Enter**.
- Press **Send** to answer, **❓ Ask** to ask a side-question without moving on.
- When you see **Next exercise →**, click it to continue.
- New words appear as colourful chips you can click to **hear** them.

Come back any time — your lessons are saved and resume where you left off.

## 🔤 Vocabulary

Every word the tutor teaches is collected here, colour-coded by gender
(**der** = blue, **die** = rose, **das** = green).

- 🔊 hear a word · 📖 **study** it in depth.
- **＋ Add word** to add your own. Don't know the article or spelling? Click **✓ Check with AI**
  and it fixes the *der/die/das*, spelling and meaning before you save.

## 🔁 Review

Quizzes you on the words that are **due**, using spaced repetition so they stick. You're shown
the meaning; type the German (with its article). The tutor grades you kindly and schedules each
word for the right day.

## 🎧 Listen (hands-free practice)

Inspired by the Paul Noble method — great for car/commute style learning.

- **Words mode:** hear the English → a pause to say the German yourself → hear the slow German.
  It loops your deck so repetition sinks in.
- **Sentences mode:** a step-by-step course for your topic. Each new word is taught on its own
  first, then built into sentences that start simple and get harder.
- Controls: **▶ Start / ⏸ Pause**, **⟲ Restart**, a **Loop** toggle, a **pause-length** slider,
  and **Show German text** if you want to read along.

## 📖 Words (deep study)

Type any German word to see:

- its **parts** and what each means (e.g. *Versicherung = ver- + sicher + -ung*),
- its **word family** (related noun/verb/adjective/adverb — click any to study it next),
- **verb conjugations**, **example sentences**, and links to **Linguee** and **Wiktionary**.

You can also reach this from any Vocabulary word with the 📖 **study** button.

## 🎤 Speak (pronunciation) — optional

Practise saying words out loud and get feedback. This is **off by default** because it needs a
microphone and a one-time download. Turn it on in **Settings → Enable microphone pronunciation**.

## ⚙️ Settings

- **API key** — change it any time.
- **Interface language**, **native language**, **German level**.
- **Voices** — pick the German and English voices used for audio (the choices depend on your
  computer).
- Toggles for speaking new words aloud, pronunciation, auto-translate, and more.

## Questions

- **“Is my data private?”** Yes — everything is stored only on your computer and never uploaded.
- **“Does it cost money?”** The tutoring uses your own Anthropic account. Normal study is a few
  cents; word studies and sentence courses are cached so you pay for each only once.
- **“It won't open on my Mac.”** Right-click the app → **Open** → **Open** (one time only).
